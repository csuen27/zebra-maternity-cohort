# zebra-maternity-cohort
This repository provides an anonymised and de-identified, study-specific dataset limited to the variables necessary to reproduce and verify the analyses reported in the study “Maternal mental health liveability shaped by composite residential environmental profiles”, accepted for publication in Nature Cities. The repository will be actively maintained and updated in response to reasonable research requests for 12 months following publication. Researchers seeking further access to the ZEBRA maternity cohort should submit a brief research proposal to the principal investigators and corresponding authors, Haitong Zhe Sun or Hefeng Huang, for review. Requests will usually receive a response within four weeks. Following approval by the ZEBRA Collaborative Committee and the Health Commission of Zhejiang Province, restricted access can be granted for research collaboration purposes for reasons of participant confidentiality and data-sharing agreements with the Chinese Medical Association (CMA).
